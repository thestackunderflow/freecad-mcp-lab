Use the solidcraft MCP tools to model a wall bracket in millimetres. It is an L shape: a base plate and a back plate, both 4 mm thick. Put the corner at the origin: width along X, depth along Y, height along Z. The base plate is 60 mm deep and lies on the XY plane (Z from 0 to 4). The back plate stands at Y from 0 to 4 and is 50 mm tall overall. Drill two 5 mm holes through the back plate, 40 mm apart, centred across the width, with their centres 35 mm above the bottom. The width is 80 mm. Make the width a parameter, so that changing it keeps the holes centred. Export the part as STEP and STL to /lab/out/bracket-80.step and /lab/out/bracket-80.stl. Then change the width to 100 mm by changing that parameter, and export /lab/out/bracket-100.step and /lab/out/bracket-100.stl. Report the bounding box after each export and any step that failed.
